A single funny line for make.fun's "One-Liner Laughs" bounty: one joke, at most 200 characters, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`. The house judge compares jokes two at a time on whether the line is a real one-line joke, how funny it is, the surprise of its turn, its freshness and its economy of wording.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It prints the line and writes it (with no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`, or to `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` when that is set.
